HYPE — division class icon generation brief

REFERENCE IMAGES
Attach rendered copies of static/neighbourhood.svg, static/map.svg,
static/globe.svg, static/sanPan.svg and static/walkingTours.svg. A rendered
reference sheet is available at docs/design/division-icons/icon-references.png.
Use neighbourhood.svg as the primary reference for the new division family.
The other assets establish the same pale monochrome colour, crisp geometry,
Hong Kong character and restrained illustrative detail. Do not use the temporary
Lucide icons currently shown beside division rows as the style reference.

MASTER PROMPT — paste into OpenAI imagegen with the reference images attached

Create a coherent family of twelve geographic division icons for HYPE, a Hong
Kong map application. Match the attached existing icons closely, especially the
solid, angular, lightly isometric building cluster in the neighbourhood icon.
Use one opaque colour (#F7E7EF) with transparent negative-space cutouts and a
fully transparent background. These are flat vector-like UI pictograms with
crisp edges, not rendered 3D objects. Match the reference's restrained oblique
perspective, straight-sided architecture and generous negative space. Architectural
icons should use filled silhouettes and a few cutout windows; landscape icons
should use equally bold, simple silhouettes and sparse contour cutouts. Use the
same visual weight, baseline, perspective and optical size throughout the family.

Each icon must be identifiable at 20–24 px. Prefer a strong silhouette and two or
three major shapes over tiny decoration. Leave generous gaps between elements.
Keep each icon centred within a square canvas with 12% clear padding. No letters,
labels, numerals, borders, badges, map pins, drop shadows, gradients, textures,
photographic detail, glow, background scenery or mockup frames. Do not bake a
checkerboard into the image. Do not add a generic circle around the subject.
Do not simply resize the same icon for different classes: distinguish classes
through composition and shape. Do not reproduce real administrative boundaries;
these are class pictograms, not geographic maps.

Generate a 4-column by 3-row concept sheet in the following exact order, with
wide transparent gutters and no labels in the image:

1. Area — three broad adjoining land parcels, with one simple internal boundary;
   a territory larger than one district, with no buildings dominating the shape.
2. District — a bounded compact urban district with three varied building blocks
   and one broad street cutout; distinct from the larger area and residential hood.
3. Town — a compact settlement with one mid-rise and two lower buildings around
   a small open centre, visibly less dense than the neighbourhood cluster.
4. Rural area — broad cultivated land bands, one low roof and a simple tree;
   landscape dominates the composition.
5. Macrohood — three small groups of buildings separated by two wide streets,
   suggesting a collection of neighbourhoods within one continuous silhouette.
6. Neighbourhood — a tight cluster of three residential buildings, directly
   related to the attached neighbourhood.svg but simplified for a 24 px row icon.
7. Microhood — one compact residential block with a small shared courtyard;
   narrower in scope than a neighbourhood, without being merely a front door.
8. Village — three low pitched-roof houses grouped along a short lane, with one
   small tree; Hong Kong village character without religious architecture.
9. Hamlet — two simple low houses with more open ground between them; no tents.
10. Major island — a broad asymmetric island silhouette, two modest hill masses
    and two short water cutouts, with land occupying most of the icon footprint.
11. Minor island — a compact island with one hill and more visible water around
    it; distinguish its composition from the major island, not just its scale.
12. Islet — a low rocky landform with two irregular rock peaks and surrounding
    water marks; clearly a small piece of land, not a shell or mountain icon.

All twelve icons must look like siblings of the supplied HYPE illustrations.
Prioritise recognisable silhouettes, clear class distinctions and consistent
weight over detail. Return the image only.

INDIVIDUAL EXPORT PROMPT — use after reviewing the concept sheet

Using the approved concept sheet and the original HYPE reference icons, produce
only the [CLASS NAME] icon, preserving its approved silhouette, perspective,
negative-space cuts and visual weight. Centre it on a 1024×1024 transparent canvas
with 12% padding. Use only opaque #F7E7EF and transparency. No caption or additional
objects. Keep it legible when reduced to 20 px. Return one transparent PNG.

DELIVERY AND REVIEW
- Set transparent_background=true when calling the image-generation tool.
- Export each approved icon individually. Use stable class filenames:
  area, district, town, rural_area, macrohood, neighbourhood, microhood, village,
  hamlet, major_island, minor_island, islet.
- Review every icon at 20, 24 and 48 px, on both near-black and pale backgrounds.
  For the pale-background check, tint the foreground dark; do not add a backdrop.
- Compare all twelve together for optical size and ensure each remains distinct.
- City and SAR are intentionally excluded from the current selectable list.
- Imagegen produces raster concepts. Rebuild/trace approved art into clean SVG
  paths with currentColor before shipping; do not embed raster images in SVG.
  Remove redundant nodes, retain transparent cutouts, and check small-size legibility
  again after conversion. This brief does not replace the current icons automatically.
